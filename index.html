Quick Start

Up and running in 5 minutes

Follow these steps in order and your budget will be ready to go.

1
🌍

Set Your Currency

Settings → Currency

Go to Settings (top-right menu) and tap your country's currency — USD, NGN, GBP, EUR, and more are supported. This changes all amounts across the whole app.

2
💰

Add Your Income

Dashboard → Income tab

Open the Dashboard and go to the Income tab. Pick the income types that apply to you — salary, freelance, rental, and more — then enter the amounts and press Calculate.

3
🧾

Enter Your Expenses

Dashboard → Expenses tab

Switch to the Expenses tab. Answer a few quick questions about housing, food, transport, and other costs one at a time, then press Calculate when done.

4
🎯

Set Savings Goals (optional)

Dashboard → Goals tab

Under the Goals tab, add a savings target with a name, amount, and deadline. The app automatically calculates how much you need to set aside each month.

5
📊

Review Your Dashboard

Dashboard → Overview

See your financial health score, budget breakdown, savings rate, and personalized tips — everything updates the moment you hit Calculate.
