About SmartBudget Planner

A free, open-source personal finance tool that helps you understand your money, set goals, and make smarter financial decisions — without ever sharing your data with anyone.

Open DashboardView on GitHub

Your Privacy is Guaranteed

SmartBudget Planner stores all your financial data exclusively in your browser's localStorage. No data is ever transmitted to any server, no accounts are required, and no analytics track your usage. Your financial information is completely private and under your control.

Features

📊

Income Tracking

Add multiple income sources and see your total monthly earnings.

💸

Expense Management

Categorize and track all your monthly expenses with easy inline editing.

🎯

Savings Goals

Set savings targets with deadlines and track your progress automatically.

📈

Financial Health Score

Get a 0-100 score based on your savings rate and spending habits.

🚨

Emergency Fund Calculator

See how much you need for 3, 6, and 12 months of expenses.

💳

Debt Payoff Estimator

Calculate how extra payments can accelerate your debt freedom date.

⚖️

50/30/20 Budget Rule

Compare your spending against the proven 50/30/20 budgeting framework.

📄

PDF & CSV Export

Download professional financial reports in multiple formats.

🌙

Dark Mode

Full dark mode support for comfortable use day or night.

🔒

Privacy First

All data stored locally in your browser — nothing sent to any server.

How to Use

1

Add Your Income

Enter your monthly salary, freelance income, or any other income sources in the Income section.

2

Track Expenses

Add your monthly expenses by category — rent, food, transport, utilities, and more.

3

Set Savings Goals

Define your savings targets with amounts and deadlines. The app calculates how much to save each month.

4

Review Your Dashboard

See your financial health score, budget breakdown, charts, and personalized tips in one view.

5

Export Reports

Download your financial summary as a PDF or CSV for record-keeping or sharing with an advisor.
